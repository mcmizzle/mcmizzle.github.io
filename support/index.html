AmbientCast and Calorie Burndown are on the App Store. Lamplighter is still pre-launch, so if you're here for that one you're probably testing it — and bug reports at that stage are more useful than they will ever be again. Send them either way.

AmbientCast

Found a bug, or have a feature request? Email support@mcmizzle.com — that reaches the developer directly. Including your Apple TV model and tvOS version helps.

Calorie Burndown

Found a bug, or have a feature request? Email support@mcmizzle.com — that reaches the developer directly. Including your device and iOS or watchOS version helps.

Lamplighter

Found a bug, or have a feature request? Email support@mcmizzle.com — that reaches the developer directly. Including your iPhone model, iOS version, and which scoreboard and interface box you're driving helps.
