AmbientCast

Live view for your Bambu printer, right on the Apple TV.

Download on the App Store

AmbientCast is a standalone tvOS app for watching your Bambu Lab printer's live camera feed and print status — no phone, no Home Assistant box, no other device in between. Add your printer's address and access code once — no Bambu account, and nothing leaves your network — and the dashboard boots straight up on every future launch.

Optional Picture-in-Picture keeps the feed floating while you use the rest of the Apple TV, and optional YouTube Live broadcasting relays the same feed outside the house.
